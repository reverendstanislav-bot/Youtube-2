- surface: shallow Louisiana lake + drilling rig;
- below: vast salt-mine chambers;

## Cold-open hypothesis

Start in parallel, not chronology:

**Surface:** the drilling crew cannot free the bit.  
**Underground:** an electrician finds water where water should not exist.  
**Surface:** the rig begins to lean.  
**Narrator:** beneath a lake only a few feet deep is an industrial void more than a thousand feet below the surface.

Then:
**"Within hours, the lake would be gone."**

Do not reveal the full canal reversal/waterfall in the first 30 seconds.

## 15–18 minute retention architecture

### 0 — Cold open: the impossible warning
- stuck drill;
- unexpected water underground;
- rig starts to tilt;
- promise: lake disappears.

### 1 — The hidden world under Jefferson Island
- salt dome;
- shallow lake;
- mine levels;
- oil above / salt below.

### 2 — Two industries share one piece of geology
- Diamond Crystal mine;
- Texaco lease;
- Wilson drilling;
- maps/permits/interface;
- introduce uncertainty without resolving blame.

### 3 — The connection
- drill problem;
- pathway opens;
- water begins entering;
- why salt changes everything.

### 4 — The mine evacuation
- electrician/alarm;
- movement through underground workings;
- elevator bottleneck;
- more than 50 escape;
- no deaths.

### 5 — The lake becomes the machine
- water dissolves / widens flow;
- rig disappears;
- barges/shoreline/land pulled inward;
- move from industrial accident to landscape event.

### 6 — The river runs backward
- lake level falls;
- Delcambre Canal reverses;
- Gulf water flows inland;
- temporary ~150-ft waterfall;
- visual climax.

### 7 — When the water stops
- transformed basin;
- destroyed mine;
- changed shoreline;
- no human deaths, but industrial/economic loss.

### 8 — Who caused it?
- Diamond vs Texaco positions;
- MSHA investigation;
- official uncertainty;
- settlement without simplifying the unresolved engineering question.

### 9 — The geology remains industrial
- jobs lost / settlements;
- today's lake/salt-dome infrastructure;
- current gas-storage context if fact check closes it;
- ending callback: the lake still sits above the same salt dome, but after 1980 the hidden system beneath it can never be treated as invisible again.
