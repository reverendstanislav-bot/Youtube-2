# Lake Peigneur — Documentary Angles V1

## Angle A — THE LAKE FELL INTO THE INDUSTRIAL WORLD BENEATH IT

Core question:
**How can a shallow lake disappear into the ground in only hours?**

Strength:
- immediate visual mystery;
- straightforward explanation through hidden mine geometry;
- very strong title/thumbnail potential.

Weakness:
- risks becoming a generic "crazy disaster" retelling.

## Angle B — TWO INDUSTRIES, ONE SALT DOME — RECOMMENDED

Core question:
**What happens when two industrial systems occupy the same geology but do not share one perfect map of the underground?**

Structure:
- surface: oil drilling;
- underground: salt mine;
- interface: uncertain;
- breach: water meets salt;
- amplification: geology turns a drilling problem into a landscape-scale failure;
- aftermath: lawsuits reveal that even after years of investigation, the precise initiating fault remained disputed.

Why this is strongest for HIA:
- industrial system story, not just disaster spectacle;
- explains the hidden infrastructure;
- makes uncertainty part of the narrative;
- naturally supports maps, mine plans, drilling diagrams, geology and documents;
- avoids overstated blame.

## Angle C — THE MIRACLE WAS UNDERGROUND

Core question:
**How did more than fifty people escape a mine while a lake was pouring into it?**

Strength:
- human tension;
- training / systems / leadership;
- strong second-act sequence.

Weakness:
- too narrow for the full episode.

Use:
Fold this into Angle B as the human centerpiece.

## Recommended thesis

> Lake Peigneur became a disaster when a surface drilling operation connected with a hidden salt mine — but the extraordinary part is that the physical failure is clearer than the human blame. Water, salt and gravity amplified the breach faster than investigators could later reconstruct exactly who had mapped what wrong.

## Tone

Not:
- "dumbest engineering mistake ever";
- comedy;
- blame-first;
- disaster porn.

Yes:
- calm forensic documentary;
- escalating physical logic;
- industrial systems;
- survival;
- evidence vs popular legend.
