## Closed / production-safe

### Event identity
- Date: **November 20, 1980**.
- Site: Lake Peigneur / Jefferson Island, Iberia Parish, Louisiana.
- Underground operator: **Diamond Crystal Salt Company**.
- Surface oil operator: **Texaco**.
- Drilling contractor: **Wilson Brothers Corporation**.

Primary control:
U.S. Department of Labor / MSHA, *The Jefferson Island Mine Inundation* (1981).

### Mine and drilling relationship
- Salt mining had been active at Jefferson Island since the early 20th century.
- Texaco held a state oil lease under Lake Peigneur and Wilson Brothers drilled the exploratory well from a floating platform.
- The failure connected lake water with the underground salt-mine system.
- MSHA's investigators reconstructed a likely drill-hole location near mined workings but explicitly did **not** claim they could conclusively determine the precise initiating cause.

## Research conclusion

The strongest evidence-led film is **not**:
> "A surveyor made one stupid coordinate mistake."

It is:
> Two industrial systems occupied the same salt dome. A drilling operation opened a hydraulic connection with a salt mine. Once fresh water reached salt, the physical failure amplified itself.
