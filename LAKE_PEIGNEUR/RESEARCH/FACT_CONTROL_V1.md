# Lake Peigneur — Fact Control V1

## LOCKED / SAFE

- Event date: **1980-11-20**.
- Diamond Crystal operated the Jefferson Island salt mine.
- Texaco was conducting oil exploration; Wilson Brothers was the drilling contractor.
- Lake water entered underground mine workings and inundated the mine.
- MSHA recorded **52 people underground** and a successful evacuation in less than one hour.
- Seven workers were reported on the drilling platform and escaped before its loss.
- No human deaths were reported.
- Delcambre Canal reversed flow during the event.
- Major litigation and settlements followed.
- Diamond Crystal settlement: **$32 million** in 1983.
- Live Oak Gardens interests: **$12.8 million** in 1983.
- Approximately **250 Diamond Crystal employees** were laid off after the mine closure, per Babin litigation record.
- MSHA did **not** conclusively establish the precise initiating cause.
