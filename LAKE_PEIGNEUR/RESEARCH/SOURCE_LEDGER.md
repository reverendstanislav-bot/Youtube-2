Rights:

## Explicit source conflicts to preserve

### Underground headcount
- MSHA FY1981 annual report: **52 people underground**.
- contemporary news commonly reported 50.
- later internet retellings often say 55.

Canonical production wording until the full MSHA incident report is parsed:
**"more than fifty people were underground; MSHA's FY1981 report gives 52."**

### Exact initiating cause
Official MSHA: **not conclusively determinable** from surviving evidence.

### Waterfall height
Common reliable range: around **150 ft**; later sources also give 164 ft.
Narration should use **"roughly 150 feet"** unless the geologic report closes the number.

### Post-event depth
Do not repeat the internet's 1,300-ft lake-depth claim. Confirm from LGS/modern bathymetry first.
