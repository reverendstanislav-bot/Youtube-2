## Visual rules
- Archive is used as evidence and atmosphere, not as a repetitive slideshow.
- Documents never occupy several consecutive visual planes.
- One narration moment gets one dominant visual idea.
- Every 10–20 seconds, change visual grammar where story permits: archive → reconstruction → map/GFX → evidence → reconstruction.
- Maps explain geography/geometry; they do not replace emotional/action visuals.
- Generated reconstructions are explicitly modern reconstructions, never fake archive.
- Subtitle-safe lower 12–15% remains clear.
- Faces, critical labels and evidence highlights stay out of caption zone.
- Appendix EE remains CONDITIONAL_USE until item-level rights are cleared.
