### Big substantive fact correction caught BEFORE release
**REJECT** prior Stage03 V1 statement "12% annual reduction in cross-sectional area", which was neither located nor reconciled in authoritative Army TR174 original. V2 has **only confirmed ARMY65 Table II measured roof-arch closure**: main trench approx 16 inches per year average, side trenches approx 34 inches per year (1962–63). Original original public 1965 report summary and details: https://www.ft.dk/samling/20131/almdel/gru/spm/72/svar/1151086/1392824/index.htm , printed page 38–40. **Do not reintroduce percentages or conflate clearance, volume and snow load**.
Original printed p41 estimates **20,000 cubic yards of snow would have to be trimmed annually** to maintain original trench cross-sections; this is an **engineering estimate**, not measured annual snow actually removed. ARMY65 printed p46 states in **summer 1963 they removed the main trench's floor and excavated another four feet** to restore clearance with utilities. V2 uses correct temporal and epistemic language for both.
