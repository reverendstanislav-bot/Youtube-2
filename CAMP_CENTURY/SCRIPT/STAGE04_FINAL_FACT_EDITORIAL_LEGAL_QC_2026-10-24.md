## Substantive V3-to-V4 fixes
1. **FALSE IMPLIED MOTIVE removed:** V3 connected the $5.7 million PM-2A cost with later planned Project Iceworm too causally. Army nuclear facility was already being planned in 1958. Iceworm formally proposed in 1962. V4 states the sequence; it does not say the missile proposal bankrolled nuclear plant construction.
2. **FIGURE SCOPE REPAIRED:** 12% cross-sectional area loss was measured for Main Trench Station 6+00; 17% for Trench 20 Station 2+60, Army 1965 p39. Neither is a universal rate for Camp Century or the entire Greenland ice sheet.
3. **DIFFERENT MEASUREMENTS KEPT APART:** Army Table II p38 reports *vertical closure* averages of 16 inches/year main and 34 inches/year certain side trenches. Inches of headroom vs percent of original area are not the same statistic.
4. **ANNUAL MAINTENANCE ESTIMATE:** 20,000 cu yards/year on Army p41 was *estimated necessary* removal to maintain the original cross sections, NOT verified as actually excavated each year.
5. **SEWAGE LOCALIZATION:** Army pp42–44 reports ~four foot settlement at the end of Trench 19 near a sewage sump, leading to removal of two T-5 buildings; it is NOT the whole city dropping four feet.
6. **TOTAL EXPENSE:** $7.92m reported actual initial construction cost, $5.7m listed for PM-2A in the historical accounting, NOT present-day dollars; no adjustment or new unaudited totals.
7. **TRIP AND CARGO:** 138 miles Camp Tuto→Century, 1k short tons in 1959 +4.6k 1960; the derived ~5.6k tons is cargo over those seasons, NOT declared weight of buildings, personal luggage or later supply.
8. **YEAR-ROUND AND FINAL SHUTDOWN:** S24 dates year-round use until summer1963, C16 dates ceasing year-round operations to1964; S24 states abandoned summer1966 while C16 dates final 1967. V4 mentions year-round disagreement and attributes 1967 to one study; do not produce a fake single authoritative closure day.
9. **NASA RADAR FIRSTNESS:** 2024 study provided a different/new view; earlier radars already detected base. V4 no longer describes an entirely new first discovery.
10. **NUCLEAR/ENVIRONMENT:** PM-2A reactor dismantled/removed, but residual wastes and infrastructure left; no active underwater reactor, radioactive catastrophe footage, or claim nuclear pollution flows into ocean today.
11. **PUBLIC VS SECRET:** the actual experimental research station was documented publicly, while Project Iceworm's vast missile proposal was separate, unbuilt. Do not describe 600 deployed missiles or 2100 built missile silos.
12. **VISUAL PROVENANCE:** lines asserting what appears in original film weakened to historically grounded descriptions of the military cameras and engineering documents; each promised actual clip must still be screened.

2. **USACE 1963 32:26 MP4** is explicitly identified by the federal owner's catalog as public domain. U.S. Copyright Act 17 USC 105 limits U.S. federal works copyright protection. Relevant official policy: https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html . However, third-party portions, music, sound recordings and derivative versions require item review; YouTube may issue Content ID or mistaken claims even with genuinely lawful content.
3. **Non-government newspaper or magazine photos** (Saturday Evening Post, National Geographic) are NOT cleared merely because a science portal has reposted them. Exclude absent independent license/rights evidence.

5. **2024 The Cryosphere scientific article** is licensed CC BY 4.0 for article text/author material, but **individual photographs explicitly credited to different creators retain their own rights**; trace original creator/license, source-credit CC BY content, and review permitted adaptations.
6. **NASA 2024 radar**: NASA generally allows appropriate use of its material subject to its media guidelines and credit/prohibited NASA-endorsement implications. Use actual NASA-owned radar graphic with full source caption, no private-use restrictions or invented external endorsement. Media rules: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ .
