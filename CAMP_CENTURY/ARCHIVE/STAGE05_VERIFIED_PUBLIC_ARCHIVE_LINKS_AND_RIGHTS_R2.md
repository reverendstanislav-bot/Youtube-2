## Rights labels — meaning for monetized YouTube
- `PD_US_GOV_OFFICIAL`: U.S. agency specifically identifies federal-produced work as public domain, or the actual work and creator federal status are clear. **Eligible SOURCE**, not a strike-proof guarantee. Verify no third-party music/footage within, save original source page, and avoid implying Army/NASA/DoD endorsement.
- `PD_US_GOV_COMMONS_ORIGINAL`: authentic U.S. agency report/photo with individual file's explicit U.S. federal PD notice on Wikimedia Commons; trace linked originating agency report. Can use original uncolored charts rather than others' transformations.
- `NASA_GOV_USE_CONDITIONS`: use original NASA-created stills per NASA Media Usage Guidelines, credit NASA, check third-party annotations, do not imply endorsement.
- `CC_BY_SA_4_REVIEW`: attribution + link to license + changes description + share-alike obligations apply; **prefer public-domain original**.
- `PRESS_ONLY / PROHIBIT_UNTIL_LICENSE`: **not cleared for a monetized standalone documentary**, even if downloadable from the internet. A press-only permission is not carte blanche for commercial reuse.
- `SOURCE_ORIGINAL_RUNTIME_UNVERIFIED`: catalogue says what film depicts, **NOT physical playback or visual QC**.
