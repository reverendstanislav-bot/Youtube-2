Replaces mechanical R2 edit decisions for 95 positions mapped to 46 locked narration blocks. Not a claim of actual acquired footage or synced audio.

## Editing principle
Every cut must (1) reveal an event, (2) develop real mechanical action, (3) change geographical scale, (4) explain a fact, or (5) land an emotional contrast. If none applies, let the film play. No regular 10-second image-switch pattern, no slideshow. Authentic film is the main surface.

## Pacing
First 60 seconds: real moving missile elevator, contrast with American city, nuclear-capable revelation. Quick 2–5s cutaways at real revelations; then genuine longer 8–20s mechanical takes. No cold-open ident.
After first minute: alternate documentary action (8–20s, sometimes 25s if actually changing) with 2–4 brisk shots at story pivots. No repeated radar-bomber-graphic rhythm.
Graphics: six unique explanatory graphics maximum, each fact-tied, no consecutive static cards, no fake historical documents or nuclear detonation.
Photos are rare and only when unique facts need proof. No irrelevant military stock. Lower 15% quiet for subtitles.
Use safe, period-authentic native aspect (4:3 footage must not be stretched to 16:9).
Sound: narration clear, real original atmosphere when licensed, restrained scoring, a music break at Hercules disclosure and ballistic-threat reversal. No fabricated alarms or cinematic explosion.
