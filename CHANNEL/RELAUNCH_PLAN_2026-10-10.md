## Analytics checkpoints

Capture the same metrics at 6h / 24h / 48h for every long-form:

- views;
- impressions;
- CTR;
- Browse;
- Suggested;
- Search;
- External;
- first-30-second retention;
- average view duration;
- average percentage viewed;
- subscribers gained;
- end-screen CTR where available.

Decision rule:
- do not panic-change packaging on microscopic samples such as 18 impressions;
- if hundreds of impressions accumulate and CTR remains roughly 0–2%, packaging becomes the primary suspect;
- if CTR is healthy but early retention collapses, focus on cold open/content rather than thumbnail.
