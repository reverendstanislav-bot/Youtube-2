# HIA four-Shorts writing framework V1.0

Authority: owner task 2026-11-04, Stage 11. Future writing packages require four independent scripts, superseding the2026-11-02 NO SHORTS planning rule and older continuous-master-audio-extraction-only writing rule. This does not authorize production, rewrite locked batches, revive old render workflows or change no-subtitles policy.

## Four independent story units

Maximum 30–35s each, including any intro/outro/CTA tail. Four crops of one discovery do not become four independent ideas. Adapt these roles to actual evidence:
1. Opening contradiction with one concrete answer.
2. One mechanism that changes understanding.
3. One real test result, limit, tradeoff or consequential decision.
4. Surprising legacy or changed purpose.

These are options, not permission to invent failure. Each has immediate hook, one independently understandable discovery, clear payoff and natural connection to the long film. The payoff cannot exist only behind a click. No stronger claim than the long form, no missing-context distortion and no need to know the project name beforehand.

## Retained production boundaries

No dialogue subtitles, karaoke or publishing SRT/VTT; internal ASR is timing only. No generated video or stock implying a specific historical event. No new images or Shorts-only graphics generated by default. Use approved assets and current accepted native vertical treatment; do not revive rejected blur-fill/small-horizontal-strip wrappers. Follow provenance rules with editorial layers, not generator labels burned into bases. Actual readability, geometry and render QC happen later; writing PASS is not rendered-Short PASS.

If fewer than four defensible discoveries exist, mark QC19 HOLD with the exact gap and revise the angle or obtain an owner exception. No invented facts or padding. No automatic schedule, upload, publication or spending follows.
