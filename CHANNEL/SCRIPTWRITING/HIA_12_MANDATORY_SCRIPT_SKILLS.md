# HIA — 12 Mandatory Scriptwriting & Editorial Skills (V1.0, 2026-11-05)

**Owner directive:** all 12 skills are mandatory for every NEW or UNLOCKED Hidden Industrial America script in Youtube-2. They are explicit editorial practices and QC checks, **not proven YouTube algorithm secrets**. Read with [HIA canon](HIA_SCRIPTWRITING_CANON.md), [template](HIA_SCRIPT_TEMPLATE.md), [20-item QC](HIA_SCRIPT_QC_CHECKLIST.md) and current owner/episode locks.

**Scope & precedence:** A skill must be deliberately applied, or documented as a justified evidence-driven alternative, not mechanically forced. Truth, chronology required for comprehension, meaningful source availability, rights, approved visual language and emotional clarity override gimmicks. Do not invent catastrophes, documents, villains, quotes, certainty, cliffhangers or algorithmic rules. No Shorts without renewed owner approval; no AI video; other established owner locks and existing Script Locks stay intact. This document does NOT authorize script changes, voice/image generation, editing, spending or publication.

## Mandatory skill set / how to apply / required evidence

| ID | Mandatory skill | Operational rule | Proof in draft/QC |
|---|---|---|---|
| SK01 | **Reverse Hook Engineering** | Open with the strongest *verified* consequence, failed task, paradox or visual action; move only essential causal context after the hook. If a later reveal would be misleading in cold open, choose the strongest honest entry instead. | Opening paragraph IDs, verified claim/visual, reason this beats chronological introduction. |
| SK02 | **Open Loop Stacking** | Establish one main investigatory question plus a limited number of smaller answerable questions; close smaller loops on schedule and keep a clear main payoff. Avoid accumulating unanswered bait. | Loop register: question, opened-at ID, expected payoff ID, actual resolution. |
| SK03 | **Retention Reset** | At natural scene/act boundaries introduce *meaningful* change: fresh evidence, decision, consequence, raised/changed stake or perspective. Inspect low-information spans; do NOT force time-based disruptions. | Retention map: distinct new fact/turn, paragraph IDs and visual/audio rationale. |
| SK04 | **Visual-First Scripting** | Before expanding a scene, identify real archive footage, photographs, document page, technical drawing or source-grounded editable graphic. Mark footage rights/quality and truthful fallback. Do not substitute generic stock for a specific historical action. | Visual feasibility map with asset/source ID, type, rights/QC status, gap and fallback. |
| SK05 | **Information Gap Ladder** | Each key answer changes understanding and naturally raises the next causal question. No endless withholding of information already known or promised. | For each act: prior question → verified answer → resulting new question. |
| SK06 | **Thumbnail-to-Hook Continuity** | First seconds visibly and verbally substantiate the headline/thumbnail's *real* promise; avoid bait-and-switch, fabricated imagery or unsupported claims. Thumbnail concept may be drafted before the script but remains fact checked. | Packaging promise, exact opening paragraph, proposed matching shot/asset and source. |
| SK07 | **Micro-Cliffhanger Editing** | Where an authentic transition permits, end a scene on a specific unresolved consequence and resolve it immediately/soon in the next; use sparingly and not at every cut. | Transition map: setup ID, next-scene payoff ID; justification if no cliffhanger. |
| SK08 | **Evidence Reveal** | On major revelations show the actual evidence (legible document, archive, photograph or data) before or alongside the explanation *when visually feasible*; then interpret accurately, with caveats. Never fabricate primary documents. | Claim ID, source pinpoint, proposed reveal order, on-screen readable detail and interpretation. |
| SK09 | **Audio-Led Editing** | Design visuals, meaningful pauses, transitions and sound around narrator meaning/emphasis; do not edit to arbitrary shot-count/beat rhythms. Spoken script remains independent from nonspoken direction. | Narration paragraph/word or cue, emphasis and visual/SFX transition notes; no unapproved VO generation. |
| SK10 | **Silent Hook** | Explicitly test whether a brief silent or near-silent true visual cold open is more powerful than voice. **Not a compulsory pause**: if it weakens clarity or promise, mark NOT SELECTED with reason and use spoken opening. | Silent-vs-voiced opening decision, asset, duration rationale; no artificial silence. |
| SK11 | **Script Compression** | Remove repeated thesis, numbers, scene setup, redundant explanations and non-causal background; preserve new evidence, caveats and required comprehension. Never cut solely to arbitrary duration. | Before/after passage IDs or compression log with repetitions removed and facts preserved. |
| SK12 | **Visual Proof Density** | Every major factual/technical assertion needs grounded on-screen support where suitable: archive, original drawing, verifiable document, measured figure, transparent explanatory diagram. Visual variety must not imply that reconstructed images are evidence. | Claim-to-visual ledger with source ID/page/time, authenticity, rights and accurate role. |

## Required workflow — new scripts only

1. **PREWRITE:** State one central question, verified high-stakes event, packaging promise, main payoff, source status and real visual opportunities; develop SK01, SK04, SK06, SK10 alternatives before locking the opening.
2. **OUTLINE:** Build act-by-act *verified* causal question ladder (SK02/SK05), mark genuine turns and resets (SK03), and candidate factual scene transitions (SK07).
3. **DRAFT:** Write strong natural narration first, keep narration separate from edit notes, source references and generated prompts. Anchor SK08/SK12 to real evidence and SK09 to narration semantics.
4. **EDIT:** Run SK11 redundancy pass and the first-minute packaging/hook proof test. Resolve every open question (or explicitly identify unknowable claims); don't exaggerate suspense.
5. **QC BEFORE SCRIPT LOCK:** Fill the 12-skill matrix below alongside existing 20-item QC; each skill requires PASS or documented justified alternative, while unresolved factual/visual holes are HOLD. Any evidence fabrication is REJECT. Skills cannot override owner approval.
