# HIA factual research and source-integrity protocol

Read [canon](HIA_SCRIPTWRITING_CANON.md). Establish evidence before drafting. Preserve ledgers, existing HOLDs and locked wording.

## Sources and claims

Prioritize primary historical documents; government/institutional archives; engineering reports/papers; museum records; contemporary reporting; academic history; specialist secondary accounts. A later institutional article is not automatically stronger than a contemporary technical report. Hosting a newspaper does not make its claims/copyright governmental.

Give every major historical, technical, quantitative, causal and comparative claim a stable claim ID and paragraph ID. Record exact claim, author/title/date, URL/item ID, PDF page or source-video time, edition/access date, supported wording, conflicts, certainty and reviewer. Distinguish exact quotation from paraphrase; verify quotes against actual scan/audio.

## Required distinctions

- Proposed specification / actual construction / demonstrated operation / commercial performance.
- Design maximum / observed test result / sustained operation; prototype / full fleet.
- Contemporary belief / later interpretation / popular legend.
- Target portion / whole object; gross/net/short/metric tons; currency/year/nominal/adjusted value.
- Departure / arrival / test window / operation / discovery / publication as different dates.
- Documented cause / allegation / correlation / unresolved mechanism.
- Recorded motive / invented thought; documented action / illustrative reconstruction.

Numbers require units, scope, date and conditions. Inspect differing definitions/component counts; record disagreements and qualify or omit precision. Never silently choose the dramatic number. Respect redactions/unknowns. No invented dialogue, performance, historical photos/documents, dates, costs, death tolls or accident causes.

## Independent final narration check

Start a separate pass from final spoken paragraphs, not already-approved research notes. Trace every factual clause, including hooks, transitions, CTA premises, graphics and Shorts, to actual support. Record extrapolations introduced in polish. A separate pass need not be another agent, but scope must be stated honestly.

Where EN/RU required, compare one-to-one IDs, meanings, negations, dates, units, uncertainty and CTAs. Clean VO equals approved EN after removing nonspoken markup only. Pronunciation notes are separate. No silent fact normalization or locked-text rewrite.

## Rights and quality remain separate

For each visual record actual file/URL/hash, creator/date, permission/licence evidence, third-party components, attribution/reuse terms, native resolution, era/identity and viewing scope. Availability, catalogue presence, download, decode and claimed PD/CC0 are not equivalent to reuse clearance.

Record independent FACT / RIGHTS / TECHNICAL / EDITORIAL PASS,HOLD,REJECT and OWNER APPROVAL. Government-funded contractor work or copied commercial images are not automatically cleared. Current $0 policy remains; no paid scans/orders or external rights messages without authorization.

Generated documents are explanatory artwork, not fabricated evidence, signatures, stamps or measurements. Exact text/quantitative graphs use verified information and editable layers. Reconstruction cannot prove an event. Label late photographs honestly; upscale does not make SD nativeHD.

## Blocking rules

REJECT demonstrably false/fabricated critical material. HOLD missing evidence, unreviewed critical claims and unresolved conflicts. Remove, qualify or correct the affected wording and repeat final-text checking. No Script Lock with unresolved critical factual error. Pending visual rights require documented feasible alternatives and a separate production HOLD; never announce all assets cleared. Owner script approval, measured audio/ASR, listening, native render, full AV review and publication are separate later gates.
