# HIA hook and retention QC

Use [canon](HIA_SCRIPTWRITING_CANON.md) and [template](HIA_SCRIPT_TEMPLATE.md). This assesses editorial construction, not measured YouTube retention or guaranteed views.

## Opening proof

Evaluate actual spoken first 15s and full first minute, not title/description:
- What verified contradiction/action is understandable without knowing the project name?
- Is the promise factual, consequential and delivered later?
- Are stakes concrete instead of a specification list?
- Does the viewer know what remains unexplained?
- Is this context necessary now, or should the question come first?
- Does the opening spoil the mechanism/repairs or close its own mystery?
- Is it free of subscription, generic introduction and fabricated suspense?

PASS needs paragraph evidence for hook, stakes, paradox and question. HOLD: function exists only in outline, is unverified or unclear/delayed. REJECT: misleading promise, fabricated event/number or unanswered central promise. Flexible15s boundaries alone cannot reject a strong hook.

## Continuous investigation

For every major chapter record new fact/event, main/subquestion, answer, causal transition, emotional basis, actual visual, repetition and slowdown risk. Explain why a choice caused the next situation. Resolve secondary loops instead of stacking unanswered questions; give useful answers throughout while preserving the main investigation.

Review progression roughly every45–75s: constraint, attempt, discovered limit, source, mechanism or implication. Flag longer unchanged stretches for review, not automatic failure. Do not create cliffhangers or use motion to conceal unchanged information. Escalate through changing understanding, not inflated numbers.

## Priority passes

1. First minute: earn curiosity without prematurely explaining the story.
2. First five minutes: reach a real decision/mechanism; background must earn its place.
3. Whole film: remove duplicated theses/specifications and interchangeable industrial visuals. Human action and mechanism carry the faceless format.
4. Last90s: answer the question, distinguish known fate/unknowns, protect emotional resolution; no promotional speech or unsupported new mystery.

Each concern needs paragraph IDs, concrete issue, required correction and retest. Preserve successful passages; do not rewrite a complete accepted script to improve a score.

## CTA and ending

Mid opportunity around 30–40%, after payoff,8–12s target; never interrupt a technical step or human loss. Final opportunity: one relevant question and natural subscribe invitation, not stacked requests. Document/review visual or pinned-comment alternatives explicitly. Verify the actual previous published film. Include spoken CTA in words/time; quiet tail separately.

## Evidence limits

A script score cannot reveal CTR, satisfaction, AVD, session time or actual audience retention. Use existing scene-specific competitor observations; channel names/transcript patterns do not prove causation. Missing reference transcript stays unverified. Human editorial verdict, structural validity and owner approval are separate. Use PASS/HOLD/REJECT, not fabricated percentages; strong pacing cannot compensate for critical falsehood.
