## Music and sound design

The V4 review master uses an **original procedural underscore generated by the build itself**. No third-party commercial music file is embedded.

The underscore is intentionally restrained:
- low industrial root/fifth pads;
- chapter-specific mechanical texture;
- deliberate dropouts / near-silence;
- paper rustles on documents;
- mechanical transients on detail shots;
- heavy-lift rotor/air wash in the Sky chapter;
- near-silence in the final aftermath.

This keeps the review independent of Content ID/licensing decisions.

For an external final-master alternative, YouTube Audio Library is the preferred source. If an Audio Library track says attribution is required, copy the supplied credit into the video description. Otherwise use the "Attribution not required" filter.
