# Actual NARA film copy procurement — verified official procedure

This replaces any casual assumption that a request email automatically yields a high-resolution film. No archive order was placed or paid for; letters are UNSENT.

**Authority:** NARA https://www.archives.gov/research/motion-pictures/order-copies and permissions https://www.archives.gov/research/motion-pictures/permissions .

1. Send title-level research enquiry to mopix@nara.gov asking staff to confirm exact local IDs, RG, reel numbers, archival holder and preview availability. Existing historical USAF numbered titles are leads, **not accepted IARL identifiers automatically**.
2. To order high-resolution copies, submit the official **NA Form 14110 Item Approval Request List (IARL)** as Word document, not a fabricated PDF. Official process currently restricts to **12 reels per IARL** and **two IARLs/week per researcher or production group** (not the same as 13 titles in the non-order research enquiry).
3. NARA evaluates each individual reel format (intermediate / preservation), availability, known restrictions, and may require third-party written permissions. It issues an approval/order number; approval valid six months.
4. Reproduction is undertaken through approved vendor and involves handling/transfer fees. **Do not charge money or initiate paid order without owner authorization.**
5. Production copy transfer usually means **whole reels rather than selected clips**; medium quality depends on original film, not promises of true native 1080p/4K.
6. Only after archived physical binary delivery: ffprobe/SHA audit, manual 1961/1964 historical frame proof and per-shot rights screening; mark direct Tory test footage PASS solely if actually visibly depicted.
7. NARA does not act as copyright licensor and cannot guarantee public-domain or commercial-use rights. Researcher is responsible.
8. For federal copies obtained from NARA, requested credit line is "Courtesy: National Archives and Records Administration".

Separate DOE Office of History inquiries can be directed to the **officially listed** history@hq.doe.gov: https://www.energy.gov/lm/history-publications . Its query is in `DOE_HISTORY_PRIMARY_PLUTO_FILM_INQUIRY_R1_2026-10-22.md`. Do not assume DOE has the films until it responds.
