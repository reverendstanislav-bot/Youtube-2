## IMPORTANT EDITS FOUND DURING STAGE 04
1. **Tory II-A stored air / runtime.** V1 wrongly implied test duration was generally less than a minute at design-point conditions. Contemporary UCRL-6590 (October 1961) says **stored-air ground tests were limited to several minutes**. Separately, LLNL's retrospective describes the May 14, 1961 **45-second** Tory II-A test. Rewrote Chapter 4 to separate these facts. Sources:
   - https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4829009 (UCRL-6590 introduction).
   - https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-E-PURL-gpo106889/pdf/GOVPUB-E-PURL-gpo106889.pdf (LLNL anniversary historical account).
2. **Test chronology / original record vs period magazine conflict.** Federal AEC official year account, *U.S. Aeronautics and Space Activities, 1964*, chapter V **printed p.63, PDF page 67 (one-based) / PDF P66 (zero-based)**, clearly reports Tory II-C **first criticality March25; May12 intermediate at simulated Mach2.8 and 10,000ft; May20 initial full power at simulated Mach2.8 and sea level; DOD's July1 decision not to pursue flight test**. July1964 *Air Force Magazine* contemporaneous copy says **May22**; document discrepancy explicitly rather than quietly falsify a source; official AEC date **May20** governs spoken script. 42 elapsed days May20→July1 arithmetic confirmed. Sources:
   - https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/presrep1964.pdf (printed p63).
   - https://www.airandspaceforces.com/article/0764pluto/ (July1964 contemporary article).
3. **Actual decision and funding dispute.** V1 had unsupported-sounding narration that strategic ICBMs alone changed the decision. Primary AEC account says decision not to pursue a **flight-test objective**, NOT a proved motive. Contemporary 1964 sources describe late-April Joint Committee funding action; **June11 House Appropriations** made further cut as no approved military weapons system or requirement was yet identified, amid debate with administration. The May1964 Congressional Record shows committee pressure to produce flight-test plan; NASA chronology corroborates House June11. Chapter7 V2 now makes this publicly documented disagreement concrete, then states strategic alternatives/environmental concerns as retrospective interpretations only. Sources:
   - https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/1964.pdf (NASA *Astronautics and Aeronautics 1964*, p213).
   - https://www.airandspaceforces.com/article/0764pluto/
   - https://www.congress.gov/88/crecb/1964/05/07/GPO-CRECB-1964-pt8-4.pdf
4. **500 megawatts unit/precision.** LLNL retrospective rounds II-C to **~500 MW**. More granular secondary sources differ in realized test measurements (461/513). V2 explicitly says **approximate reactor thermal heat scale**, not precise test figure, thrust, or an electric plant rating. No 461, 513 or "MW of thrust" narration. Source: LLNL anniversary.
5. **Project Pluto genuine locomotive/rail industrial infrastructure.** Confirmed original contemporary UCRL-7036 Part1 p4: reactor test vehicle moved by remote-controlled flatcar between test station and disassembly building; remote site control.
