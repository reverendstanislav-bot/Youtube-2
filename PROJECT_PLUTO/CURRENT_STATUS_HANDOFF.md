## Ground truths already supported
- Project Pluto was a US Air Force / Atomic Energy Commission nuclear-ramjet development program.
- Livermore designed/tested Tory II-A and Tory II-C on the ground at the Nevada Test Site; a SLAM flight did not occur.
- Initial Tory II-A test: May 14, 1961 (45 seconds in LLNL retrospective).
- In May 1964 Tory II-C achieved successful simulated-flight-condition ground tests.
- July 1, 1964 DOD communicated that it would not pursue a Pluto flight-test objective; project phased out. Official NASA-hosted 1964 Presidential Report on AEC activities.
- Best central question: How did a machine that worked lead to a weapon that never flew?
