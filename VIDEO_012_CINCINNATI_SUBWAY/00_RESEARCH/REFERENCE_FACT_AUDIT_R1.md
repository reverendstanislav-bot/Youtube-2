# Supplied-reference fact audit R1

The attached Russian text is a style reference, not a source. This audit prevents its strongest but unsupported claims from entering HIA narration.

| Reference claim | Decision | Evidence / correction |
|---|---|---|
| Cincinnati built an unfinished subway beneath Central Parkway | PASS | City of Cincinnati DOTE documents more than two miles of unused tunnel and Central Parkway above it. |
| The 1916 bond was $6 million | PASS | City DOTE and Cincinnati Public Library. The referendum passed 39,726 to 6,652. |
| Construction began in 1920 and funds were exhausted by 1927 | PASS WITH NUANCE | Contracts began in 1919; major construction followed in 1920. Funds ran out in 1927. |
| Trains never ran | PASS | No rails or equipment were installed and crucial connections remained incomplete. |
| It was the largest unfinished subway in the New World | REJECT | No authoritative evidence found; unnecessary superlative. |
| Costs doubled during construction | HOLD | Post-WWI inflation is documented, but the exact narrated multiplier requires a dated estimate comparison. |
| Subsidence cracked buildings and triggered lawsuits | HOLD | Not established by the reviewed primary/official sources. |
| The bond was paid off in 1966 and total cost was $13,019,982.45 | PASS | Exact figures appear on the City of Cincinnati DOTE history page. |
| Batman Forever seriously planned a tunnel sequence and the city refused because ventilation work would damage the system | HOLD / REMOVE | Secondary sources mention scouting or consideration. The detailed refusal story and reason remain unverified. |
| DARPA secretly proposed testing robots and drones in the tunnels | REJECT | No evidence found. DARPA's public underground-site search was a general 2019 Subterranean Challenge request, not a verified Cincinnati deal. |
| A 1,320 mm steel water main was installed in the 1950s | REJECT / CORRECT | GCWW documents a 48-inch reinforced-concrete transmission main installed in 1957 in the west tube. |
| Telecom companies leased the tunnel in the late 1980s and it carries major state data-center traffic | HOLD | Communications cables are reported, but the date, operators, traffic, and claimed statewide importance are not established. |
| The tunnels are fully dry, welded shut, motion-monitored and guarded around the clock | HOLD / REMOVE | Some entrances are sealed and access is restricted. The stronger operational claims are not supported. |
| The city is again studying reuse | PASS | City procurement lists a Subway Tunnel Feasibility Study, awarded in 2025. |
