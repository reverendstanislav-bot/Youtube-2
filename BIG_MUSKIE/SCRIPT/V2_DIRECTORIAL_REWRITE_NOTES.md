# BIG MUSKIE — V2 DIRECTORIAL REWRITE NOTES

Status: **REVIEW NOTES — 2026-09-23**

## Why V1 was not strong enough as a film

V1 was factually disciplined but too explanatory. Its main weaknesses:
- intrigue resolved too cleanly and too early;
- human scale arrived late;
- too many specifications arrived in clusters;
- engineering was described rather than staged as action;
- the 1977 operating study was not treated strongly enough as a midpoint reversal;
- shutdown chapter was intellectually correct but not dramatically structured;
- 1999 dismantling passed too quickly for the emotional climax it should be;
- ending had the right thesis but did not fully transform the opening image.

## V2 dramatic architecture

### Act I — WHY WOULD ANYONE NEED THIS?
Opening object: surviving bucket.
Mysteries:
1. what needed a bucket like this?
2. why did the mine build a 27m-lb solution?
3. why scrap it if it actually worked?

Chapters 0–2 delay the complete engineering explanation until the economic/geologic pressure is clear.

### Act II — HOW THE ANSWER WORKED
Chapters 3–4 stage the machine as a sequence of actions:
bucket → drag → hoist → swing → dump → walk.
Technical facts appear only when the viewer needs them to explain an observed action.

Human/infrastructure scale enters before the midpoint.

### MIDPOINT REVERSAL — SCALE HAS A PRICE
Chapter 5 first proves that Big Muskie delivered enormous output, then reveals the 1977 study-month 35% mechanical availability.
This is explicitly scoped and used as a dramatic reversal, not a lifetime reliability claim.

### Act III — THE ANSWER STOPS FITTING THE QUESTION
Chapter 6 removes the supports under the 1960s economic equation one by one:
technology + high-sulfur-coal economics + changed emissions framework.
No single villain and no "EPA killed it" mythology.

### CLIMAX — THE MACHINE BECOMES THE PROBLEM
Chapter 7 treats preservation/dismantling as the final engineering/economic problem.
The object built to solve a mine problem now creates a reclamation/liability/preservation problem of its own.

### ENDING — SAME BUCKET, NEW MEANING
Opening: bucket is a scale curiosity.
Ending: bucket is evidence of an entire vanished industrial equation.

## Information-release changes from V1

- Full dimensions moved out of the opening.
- 220 yd³ / 240-ton / 325-ton bucket stats are revealed at the moment the bucket becomes the engineering center.
- Motor counts arrive only after one complete digging cycle is visualized.
- Walking mechanics begin as wonder and end as infrastructure constraint.
- 35% study-month availability is held until after productivity has been proven.
- Clean Air Act timing is used to destroy the simplistic one-law explanation.
- Dismantling is expanded into a full emotional chapter.

## Visual rhythm targets

No more than two similar machine hero shots consecutively.
Preferred progression:
present bucket → landscape → power plant/documents → mine geometry → logistics → first full machine reveal → bucket action → technical machinery → walking mechanism → crews/cable/system → productivity evidence → USBM document → idle machine → dismantling/preservation documents → bucket return → reclaimed landscape.

## Human-scale rule

Do not invent a protagonist.
Use documented human evidence:
- crews;
- supervisor Eddie Lindimore recollection, explicitly attributed;
- workers only where authentic rights-cleared archive supports them.
Human scale is used to make engineering legible, not to fabricate melodrama.
