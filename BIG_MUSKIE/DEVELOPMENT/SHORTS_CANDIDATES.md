## SHORT 01 — The bucket that survived

Target: 35–50 s

Hook:
"Almost the entire 27-million-pound machine was scrapped. Its biggest surviving public piece is this bucket."

Setup:
220-cubic-yard bucket; scale against people/vehicles.

Payoff:
The bucket survived the 1999 dismantling and anchors Miners' Memorial Park; smaller components also survive in specialist collections.

Long-form placement:
Chapter 0 or Chapter 7/8 callback.

## SHORT 02 — How Big Muskie walked

Target: 45–60 s

Hook:
"How do you move a machine that weighs more than 27 million pounds?"

Setup:
Tub + walking mechanism; enormous travel-path requirements.

Payoff:
The walker lifted/repositioned the machine, while its scale forced extremely wide, low-grade, stable travel routes.

Long-form placement:
Chapter 3.

## SHORT 03 — The 28 documented hoist/drag/swing motors

Target: 45–60 s

Hook:
"Three of Big Muskie's main motions were driven by 28 documented electric motors: hoist, drag and swing."

Setup:
Hoist / drag / swing motor groups from Bucyrus retrospective.

Payoff:
The machine becomes understandable as three coordinated motion systems; do not claim 28 was the count of every motor on the machine.

Long-form placement:
Chapter 3.

## SHORT 04 — Why Big Muskie stopped in 1991

Target: 45–60 s

Hook:
"The usual story is that one law killed Big Muskie. The documented story is more complicated."

Setup:
Changing mining technology + high-sulfur-coal market pressure after the 1990 Clean Air Act Amendments.

Payoff:
The machine was idled because the economic system that justified it had changed, not because of a single switch.

Long-form placement:
Chapter 6.

## SHORT 05 — Why they scrapped the largest dragline

Target: 45–60 s

Hook:
"If it was historic, why didn't anyone save the whole machine?"

Setup:
Preservation ideas existed; AEP faced reclamation obligations, a limited preservation window/funding problem, and long-term liability.

Payoff:
The giant was dismantled in 1999; the bucket became the surviving monument.

Long-form placement:
Chapter 7.
