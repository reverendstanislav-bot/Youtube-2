# BIG MUSKIE — RECONSTRUCTION STYLE CANON V1

Status: **LOCKED**

Applies to all generated Big Muskie historical reconstructions.

## Visual language

- Hidden Industrial America visual canon.
- Cinematic 16:9 industrial documentary reconstruction.
- Historically grounded; serious and restrained.
- Naturalistic lighting.
- Palette/grade: charcoal, iron gray, warm paper, ivory, faded rust, blueprint gray-blue.
- Preserve real Bucyrus-Erie 4250-W Big Muskie geometry from verified reference images.
- No fake archival film border, scratches or dust.
- No neon, horror, glossy concept-art look, giant graphic cards or orange collage blocks.
- No invented readable logos or signage.
- No wheels or crawler tracks on Big Muskie.
- Cables, boom, tub, bucket and machinery relationships must remain physically plausible.
- Every generated historical frame is labelled **AI RECONSTRUCTION** in the edit.
- A generated frame is never labelled **HISTORICAL SOURCE**.

## Subtitle-safe composition

Every generated frame must reserve the **bottom 12–15% of the full 16:9 frame** as visually quiet low-detail negative space.

Do not place in that lower band:
- faces;
- key machinery junctions;
- bucket;
- critical action;
- essential labels/information.

This subtitle-safe rule is mandatory for all future Big Muskie generated images.
