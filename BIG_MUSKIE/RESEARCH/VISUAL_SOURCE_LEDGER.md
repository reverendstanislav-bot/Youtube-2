## Important rights conclusions

1. **We already have at least two genuinely public-domain historical Big Muskie photographs** through EPA DOCUMERICA / NARA. These are stronger than generating fake archival photographs.
2. Commons has usable CC photographs, but the exact license/attribution for each file must be recorded.
3. Milwaukee Public Library/Bucyrus scans are excellent research documents but **not automatically publication-cleared**. MPL explicitly requires advance permission for distribution/publication of Special Collections material, and individual items may remain in copyright.
4. HCEA has authentic surviving Big Muskie components and is a priority archive-contact lead, not a free-media source by default.
5. Search-engine thumbnails and Google Images are never provenance.
