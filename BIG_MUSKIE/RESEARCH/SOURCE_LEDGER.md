## Source-quality warning list

- **Guinness:** acceptable for record category, not for lifetime-volume number; its displayed lifetime volume is inconsistent by orders of magnitude with AEP/ODNR and even its own Panama-Canal comparison.
- **Noble County tourism:** useful for current visitor context, but several machine figures are derivative/inconsistent; never prefer it over ODNR/AEP/Bucyrus.
- **WOSU oral history:** excellent human source; recollections require attribution.
- **Caterpillar history:** strong corporate historical overview, but its 330-ft boom conflicts with several Ohio/ODNR sources.
- **MPL Bucyrus scans:** excellent research evidence; visual publication rights are not automatically granted.
