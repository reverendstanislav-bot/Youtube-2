## Global anti-reject rules

These rules apply to every replacement in this pack.

1. **ONE beat = ONE standalone 16:9 image.**
   - no collage;
   - no storyboard;
   - no multi-panel contact sheet;
   - no scene numbering inside the image.

2. **Do not invent historical evidence.**
   - generated images are `HIA EXPLANATORY GFX`;
   - never imitate an official scanned EPA page, seal, signature, archival stamp or patent page unless the real source image is attached and the task is an edit;
   - never render generated imagery as `HISTORICAL SOURCE`.

3. **Big Muskie geometry lock.**
   - Big Muskie = Bucyrus-Erie 4250-W walking dragline;
   - no crawler tracks;
   - no road wheels;
   - no rail undercarriage;
   - broad circular tub/base and walking-shoe system when the base is visible;
   - if correct walking geometry is not necessary to the beat, crop the undercarriage out instead of inventing it.

4. **Technical-document lock.**
   - D03/D04 frames may NOT be generated from text alone;
   - the actual high-resolution patent figure/crop must be attached as the source image;
   - edit/enhance the source, do not redraw engineering geometry;
   - preserve every visible mechanical relationship and line placement;
   - do not add dimensions, patent text, numbered parts, mechanisms or labels that are not visible in the source.

5. **Text minimization.**
   - generated typography is limited to a headline and at most 1–3 very short labels;
   - exact dates, source citations and longer factual sentences should be added in Remotion after generation;
   - if model-rendered text is uncertain, leave the designed text area blank rather than inventing words/numbers.

6. **HIA style lock.**
   - Charcoal #171A1C
   - Iron #30363A
   - Warm paper #E6DDC8
   - Ivory #F3EBDD
   - Faded rust #A55235
   - Blueprint gray-blue #5F747D
   - premium documentary frame;
   - no neon, horror, cartoon infographic, cheap orange boxes, generic presentation UI.

7. **Subtitle safe.**
   - keep lower 12–15% free of critical information.
