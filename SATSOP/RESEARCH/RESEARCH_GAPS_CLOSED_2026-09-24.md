## Closed scale and design

- Each permitted Satsop unit was approximately 1,240 MW net and used a pressurized-water reactor.
- WNP-3 used Combustion Engineering's System 80 design and a Westinghouse turbine generator.
- EFSEC material identifies each unit's reactor, turbine generator, natural-draft cooling-tower system, reactor auxiliary building, transmission/service lines, and associated facilities.
- Later EFSEC material identifies the two cooling towers as approximately 496 feet high.

Use `about 500 feet` in narration. Do not attach a superlative without a separate comparative source.

## Closed completion and cost language

- WNP-5: about 8 percent complete in January 1980; about 14 percent at termination in January 1982.
- WNP-3: 74 percent physically complete in BPA's 1994 record; Washington legislative staff rounded this to approximately 75 percent.
- WNP-3 investment: approximately $2.6 billion in 1993 dollars, including the investor-owned utilities' 30 percent share.
- BPA's 1994 record estimated another $1.55 billion to complete WNP-3, before financing effects described elsewhere in the comparison.
- Projects 4 and 5 together: $2.25 billion of bonds sold by termination; estimated combined completion cost around $12 billion at that point.

Do not add these figures together. They use different scopes and dollar bases.

## Closed financing distinction

- WNP-1 and 70 percent of WNP-3 used the BPA net-billing structure.
- Four investor-owned utilities held the remaining 30 percent of WNP-3; a later settlement changed BPA's cost exposure.
- Projects 4 and 5 were financed through participating utilities with take-or-pay obligations.
- The famous Project 4/5 default does not describe WNP-3's financing history.

## Closed decision mechanism

BPA's 1994 comparison found:

- combined-cycle generation cheaper than the base and high WNP-3 completion cases;
- the low WNP-3 case comparable on life-cycle cost;
- WNP-3 still exposed BPA to much larger capital-at-risk, decommissioning, waste, regulatory, and operating risks.

Production interpretation: **physical completion is not the same as economic completion**.

## Closed afterlife

- The nuclear units never operated and were unfueled.
- The redevelopment transfer preserved economic value in land, buildings, utilities, water, transmission access, and industrial infrastructure.
- Current Port material describes an active, community-owned mixed-use business park.
- The turbine building is marketed as 300,000 square feet of industrial space.
- Existing tunnels and the reactor building are used for specialized training.
- Project 3 debt-related costs continued after physical transfer; Energy Northwest's FY2021 budget still contained Project 3 debt funding requirements.
