## Source-quality rules

- Tier A: contemporaneous or controlling government/owner record.
- Tier B: later official history or current public-owner record.
- Discovery-only: useful lead that cannot carry a narration claim alone.
- Dollar figures retain their source year and project scope.
- A source about WNP-3 must not be generalized to WNP-5, or vice versa.
