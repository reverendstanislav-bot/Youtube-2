Two unfinished cooling towers rise over western Washington. They look like one abandoned nuclear plant—but they belong to two projects that failed for different reasons, twelve years apart.

This episode of Hidden Industrial America follows Washington Nuclear Projects 3 and 5 at Satsop: the five-reactor expansion plan behind them, the financing structures that separated them, the 1983 bond default, and Bonneville Power Administration's 1994 decision that a 74%-complete reactor was still too risky and expensive to finish.

Neither Satsop unit entered commercial service, and the site was never fueled. Today, parts of the former nuclear project operate as the Satsop Business Park, where industrial buildings, utilities, tunnels, and training spaces have found new uses.
