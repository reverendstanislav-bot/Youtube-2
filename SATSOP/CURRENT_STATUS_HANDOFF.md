## Current documentary direction

Working thesis:

> Satsop looks like one abandoned nuclear plant, but its two unfinished units reveal two different failures: one project hit a financing wall, while the other survived in suspended animation until a later power-market decision made completion irrational.

The film should explain the system behind the ruins:

1. why the Pacific Northwest expected to need several large nuclear stations;
2. how WPPSS attempted to build five projects at once;
3. why WNP-5 and WNP-3 had different financing and termination paths;
4. why WNP-3 could be 74 percent complete and still lose to a combined-cycle alternative;
5. how the public infrastructure was transferred and reused rather than simply abandoned.

## Established facts

- Satsop hosted WNP-3 and WNP-5; it was not a single two-unit project with one financial history.
- WNP-3 was designed as a 1,240 MW pressurized-water reactor using Combustion Engineering's System 80 design.
- WNP-5 was also rated at 1,240 MW in contemporaneous GAO material.
- WNP-5 and WNP-4 were terminated on January 22, 1982. GAO reported WNP-5 at about 14 percent complete and $2.25 billion of bonds sold for Projects 4 and 5 together.
- WNP-3 construction was suspended in July 1983 and preserved for possible completion.
- BPA's 1994 Record of Decision described WNP-3 as 74 percent complete, with approximately $2.6 billion invested in 1993 dollars.
- BPA selected termination after comparing completion with combined-cycle generation and judging WNP-3's remaining capital and business risks unacceptable.
- Project 3 was terminated in 1994. Its NRC construction permit was later revoked in 1999.
- Public agencies formed the Satsop Redevelopment Project in 1995; official records describe the transfer in 1999 and report its completion during fiscal year 2000.
- The present Satsop Business Park reuses parts of the site for industry and training. The Port's own material identifies the 300,000-square-foot turbine building as an industrial/manufacturing facility.

## Critical wording controls

- Do not say both Satsop reactors were cancelled in 1982.
- Do not attribute the 1983 Project 4/5 bond default to WNP-3.
- Do not call either unit an operating reactor, a decommissioned power plant, or a nuclear accident site. Neither Satsop unit operated.
- Do not use the 74–75 percent figure for WNP-5; it belongs to WNP-3.
- Do not treat all quoted dollar figures as nominally comparable. Preserve the source year and scope.
- Do not claim the whole site is abandoned. It is an active business park, and restricted structures are not open to the public.
- Do not frame the present cooling towers as proof of radioactive contamination. The projects never operated; any environmental/remediation claim needs its own source.
