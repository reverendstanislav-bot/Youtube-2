## Fact pitfalls to prevent clickbait legal failure
- Ajax conventional vs Hercules nuclear-capable; not all sites carried nuclear warheads.
- anti-aircraft missile defense is not the same as strategic ICBM silo.
- separate subsurface magazines/elevators and fire control by distance; do not show launch rockets fired from buried shafts.
- specific NPS museum pages say locations were visible / public tours; do not call every base TOP SECRET.
- Different count definitions NPS 300 sites, >250 batteries, 19 NYC, ~20 Chicago; do not combine as one exact number.
- Closures mostly early/mid-1970s continental US, sites in Alaska/Florida survived until 1979; not all 1974 shutdown.
