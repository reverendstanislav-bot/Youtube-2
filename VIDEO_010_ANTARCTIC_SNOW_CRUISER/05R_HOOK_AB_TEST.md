## A — THE BROKEN RAMP

The wooden ramp starts to break beneath the Snow Cruiser. Thomas Poulter is at the controls, bringing his enormous Antarctic vehicle down from the ship. He applies power and gets it onto the ice. The machine survives its arrival.

Its body is more than fifty feet long. Its tires tower over people standing beside them, and its roof has space for an airplane. Its job is to take a scientific expedition through Antarctica with its home and laboratory on board.

But getting off the ship turns out to be the easier problem. Once ashore, the Cruiser struggles to make useful progress across the snow it was built to cross.

How did a machine designed to carry an entire base end up becoming a base that could barely travel?

Оценка: Выбран. Действие начинается в первой строке; выезд на лёд сразу меняет смысл спасения машины. Главный вопрос о транспортной миссии возникает до ответа о шинах.

Источники/границы: C001–C004; TIME40, PH10/PH14, D03, GOV55

## B — THE OHIO CREEK

Before the Snow Cruiser reached Antarctica, it ended up nose-first in a creek in Ohio.

This was a machine built to cross gaps in polar ice. Now its crew had to recover it from beside an American road. They did get it out, repaired it, and continued toward the ship waiting in Boston.

The strange part is that this was not the failure that ended its mission.

The Cruiser made it south. It survived another dangerous moment getting off the ship. Only then did the expedition discover how difficult it would be to take its traveling laboratory anywhere useful.

Why did the crew keep saving this enormous machine, and what could it still do when its journey failed?

Оценка: Сильная альтернатива: понятная авария и узнаваемый разрыв между назначением и реальностью. Проигрывает A по наличию готового разрешённого визуала: отдельный кадр аварии/эвакуации пока не закрыт.

Источники/границы: C020–C025; OSU, FR86, TIME40; без размера толпы и точной причины съезда

## C — THE PIT IN THE SNOW

In January 1958, visitors dug down through Antarctic snow to reach an abandoned vehicle.

A later scientific study describes the Snow Cruiser as well preserved. It had once been intended to carry a home, a laboratory, and an airplane through the Antarctic landscape. Now reaching it required digging from above.

Its original expedition was long over. Yet finding the machine did not settle its fate, because the ice carrying it could move and break away.

How had an expedition's traveling base become a buried room? And how could a vehicle disappear from a coastline after it had stopped traveling?

The answers begin with the attempts to make it move, and end with the ground that would not stay still.

Оценка: Работает как загадка позднейшей судьбы, но смещает обещание фильма с провала транспортной миссии на исчезновение. Нужна явная атрибуция исследованию2005 и честная датировка визуала.

Источники/границы: C051,C053–C055; SC05 PDF8/14. Оригинал1958 HOLD; нельзя обещать найденные сегодня обломки.
